# ZenBean release history

## Future consideration

- Android APK port with the retro mini-player, offline sound modes, touch-based music regeneration and lock-screen playback controls.

## 0.3.9 · 2026-11-15

- Shaped the chin into the sliding tray itself and cut a matching recess into the fixed shell.
- Restored the full circular play control and matched the Vista bean icon to the LCD blue.

## 0.3.8 · 2026-11-15

- Shortened the case, rounded the tray shoulders, and blended the moving chin control into the outline.
- Slowed the classical marquee and fixed clipping so the complete composer and title scroll into view.
- Added public-domain solo piano pieces by Beethoven, Mozart, Brahms, Schubert and Schumann. Classical Piano reshuffles after all seven composers play and avoids repeats across those boundaries.
- Replaced Waterfall with Engine Room, recorded on the Cape May–Lewes Ferry. Sound buttons use short labels, with details and credits in About.

## 0.3.7 · 2026-11-15

- Matched the sliding tray to the player's rounded outline and moved the toggle onto the chin, where it travels with the tray.
- Reworked Baikal and Cicadas with new Freesound mixes and added Busy City.
- Added Classical Piano with complete public-domain preludes by Bach and Chopin, stored as note data and played on a sampled grand. Long composer and piece names scroll on the display.
- Added generated Acoustic Guitar with fingerpicked nylon-string samples.

## 0.3.6 · 2026-11-15

- Saxophone Jazz and Harmonica Jazz use level-matched recordings of a tenor saxophone and a Hohner Super 64 chromatic harmonica, mixed below the piano. The two modes rebuild their cached audio.
- Shortened the rounded player shell and raised its narrower toggle with a centered line. The sliding sound tray matches the width of the shell and has a gloss rim that follows the theme.

## 0.3.5 · 2026-11-15

- The bottom bar slides open to reveal three rows of sound buttons. Click the bar again to close the panel, or let it close after a minute without a sound-button press.
- Added Saxophone Jazz and Harmonica Jazz with independently saved playback positions.
- Added Waterfall, River, Mountain, Jungle, Tunnel, Baikal, Boat, Field and Tokyo Cicadas as offline soundscapes. Each has an icon and a hover description. See the [recording credits](assets/CREDITS.md).
- Bean cycles through browns, Grass through greens and Vista through blues. Viz cycles through the full accent palette on its charcoal base.

## 0.3.4 · 2026-11-14

- Moved theme controls to the display. Bean, Grass, Vista and high-contrast Viz each remember their own hue from 64 steps.

## 0.3.3 · 2026-11-14

- Added a compact About window from Settings with a quick-start guide, the full version number and recording credits.

## 0.3.2 · 2026-11-14

- Forest uses AudioPapkin’s “Forest ambience” from Pixabay in a 125-second offline loop.

## 0.3.1 · 2026-11-14

- Forest uses a windscreen-protected recording from the Sian Ka’an Biosphere Reserve.
- Holding Jazz or Lo-fi for three seconds opens the five-second mouse movement prompt. Music rendering and the progress ring begin after the prompt.
- Original Jazz and Lo-fi pieces have an introduction, repeating theme, contrasting middle, return and resolved ending. Jazz uses sparse swing phrasing and ii–V–I cadences. Lo-fi uses soft keys, a swung backbeat and a recurring melodic hook.
- Playback begins with one rendered piece. The next piece renders in the background and takes over at the seven-minute boundary. The player retains separate music positions and caches the current and next piece for each mode.

## 0.3 · 2026-11-14

- Wind Outside uses a real indoor-window recording with softened gusts. Forest uses a woodland recording focused on distant branches, with quieter bird calls.
- The five-second “Shake the cursor (mouse pointer)” prompt incorporates mouse movement into the seed. Both music modes use that seed to select chord progressions and compose lead phrases with different pitches and rhythms.
- The title shows “ZenBean 0.3” and the Coffee theme has a darker bean.

## 0.2.3 · 2026-11-14

- Music fades to silence at each seven-minute transition before the next arrangement enters. Jazz and Lo-fi save their playback positions independently.
- Hold Jazz or Lo-fi for three seconds to regenerate. A five-second cursor movement prompt contributes to a fresh random seed.
- Added sampled Forest ambience and a muffled, howling Wind Outside. Ambience displays a loop status and an infinity symbol.
- Settings includes a themed Donate button linking to Buy Me a Coffee.

## 0.2.2 · 2026-11-14

- Jazz and Lo-fi each cycle through four distinct lead arrangements at seven-minute playback intervals. Tempo and beat phase carry through the one-second transitions.
- Rain on Glass, Rain on a Metal Roof, and Sea Waves on the Shore use embedded CC0 field recordings. See [audio credits](assets/CREDITS.md).
- Windows file properties report the version as 0.2.2.

## 0.2 · 2026-11-14

- Compact player, gentler volume curve, and level-matched tracks.
- Hold a sound button for three seconds to regenerate that mode and save its variation.
- Separate Lo-fi arrangement and revised synthesized ambience.
